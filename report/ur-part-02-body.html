Bab 1

Ye Dashboard Kya Hai

Sabse pehle ye samajhna zaroori hai ke ye cheez hai kya, aur kis masle ka hal hai.

1.1 Aasan lafzon me

Power Monitor aik website jaisa system hai jo batata hai ke poore complex me bijli kahan aur kitni kharch ho rahi hai. Ye complex 360 acre ka hai, is me 2,157 buildings hain, 3 bijli ke substation hain aur 109 transformers hain.

Dashboard par 110 jagahen monitor hoti hain. Har jagah ka apna code hota hai — jaise TW-01 (Tube Well No.1) ya MED-HOSP (PNS Hafeez Hospital). Har jagah ki aik limit set hai. Jab koi jagah apni limit se zyada bijli kharch kare, to dashboard turant alarm deta hai, aur wo alarm tab tak screen par rehta hai jab tak koi banda usay dekh kar "Ack" (acknowledge) na kar de.

1.2 Ye kis masle ko hal karta hai

Aise bare complex me pehle sirf ye pata hota hai ke kya kya cheezen maujood hain — 980 street lights, 84 motor pumps, 14 tube wells. Lekin teen sawalon ka jawab nahi milta:

  • Bijli ja kahan rahi hai? Kaun se zone aur kaun si building sabse zyada le rahi hai.
  • Abhi koi kharabi to nahi? Agar koi pump normal se double bijli le raha hai to uska bearing kharab ho raha hai — aur ye kisi ko pata nahi chalta jab tak wo jam na ho jaye.
  • Nayi building lagane ki gunjaish hai? Kya substation me itni capacity bachi hai.

Dashboard ye teenon sawalon ka jawab har waqt deta rehta hai.

1.3 Sabse zaroori baat — REG aur SIM

Ye parhe bagair koi number istemal na karein

Jo document is project ki bunyaad hai (AOR - GE (N) Maint Isld.docx), wo aik asset register hai — yaani us me sirf ye likha hai ke kya kya maujood hai aur kitni tadaad me. Us me bijli ki koi reading nahi hai.

Hum ne document me search kiya: voltage, frequency, power factor, kWh, meter, generator — in me se aik lafz bhi document me nahi hai. Sifar (zero) natija.

Isliye dashboard par jo live numbers chal rahe hain wo asli meter se nahi aa rahe — wo banaye gaye (simulated) hain. Lekin structure asli hai.

Bab 2

Bijli ke Basic Alfaz

Ye words dashboard par baar baar aate hain. Pehle aasan matlab, phir thoda technical.

2.1 Power aur Energy — sabse zyada confusion yahan hoti hai

Aik misaal se samjhein

Power (kW) gaari ke speedometer jaisa hai — abhi is waqt kitni tez bijli istemal ho rahi hai.

Energy (kWh) gaari ke meter (odometer) jaisa hai — ab tak kitni bijli total istemal ho chuki hai. Bill isi ka aata hai.

Energy = Power × Time Misaal: aik pump 10 kW ka hai aur 5 ghante chala. Energy = 10 kW × 5 ghante = 50 kWh

4. Voltage & Frequency

Frequency ka graph (49.5–50.5 Hz ki limit ke saath), teen substation ke alag alag voltage graph, aur aik table jis me har substation ka voltage, imbalance, current aur power factor hai. Sabse zaroori number: Phase Imbalance — 2% se kam rehna chahiye.

5. Energy Analytics

Kitni energy kharch hui, average kitna load raha, peak kab tha, load factor kitna hai. Aik gol chart me: grid se li gayi, solar se bani, aur losses. Aik khaas graph "Load Duration Curve" — ye batata hai ke kitne waqt tak peak ke qareeb rahe. Neeche top 10 sabse zyada kharch karne wali jagahen.

6. Solar

13 solar system ki performance. Sabse ahem number "Performance Ratio" hai — yaani jitni bijli abhi ke suraj me banni chahiye thi, us ka kitna percent asal me bani. Subah kam output normal hai; dopahar me kam output kharabi hai. Sirf ratio se ye farq pata chalta hai.

7. Transformers

Ye akela tab hai jis par kuch bhi banaya hua nahi hai — sab kuch register se hai. 109 transformer, 18,530 kVA, aur har substation par kitne lage hain.

8. Switchgear

8 breakers (VCB) ki halat: Closed (chal raha hai), Standby (spare), Open (band kiya gaya), Tripped (kharabi par khud band ho gaya). Neeche 475 distribution boards ka summary.

9. Alarms & Events

Sare active alarm, aur poori history. Har alarm ke teen waqt likhe hote hain: kab hua, kab dekha gaya, kab theek hua. Ye teen waqt kisi bhi incident ke baad sabse zaroori sawal ka jawab dete hain.

10. Reports

Session ka summary aur do CSV export buttons (Excel me khul jate hain): aik sari jagahon ka, aik alarm history ka.

11. Forecasting

Agle 24 ghante ka andaza. Solid line = jo ho chuka, dotted line = jo hone ka andaza hai. Shaded (halka) hissa confidence range hai — aage jaa kar chaura hota jata hai kyunki door ka andaza kam pakka hota hai.

Forecast ke baare me sach

Ye asli "trained forecast" nahi hai. Ye sirf har group ke rozana ke pattern ko jorr kar banaya gaya andaza hai, kyunki purana record maujood hi nahi hai jis se model seekhta. Jab kuch hafton ka asli data jama ho jaye ga, tab is ko proper model se badla ja sakta hai. Ye baat dashboard par bhi likhi hui hai.

12. Settings

Poori tafseel ke kaun sa number kahan se aaya. Agar koi banda kisi figure par sawal uthaye to ye tab kholein.

Bab 4

Alarm System Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Ye dashboard ka sabse ahem hissa hai, aur is me kaafi soch bichar ki gayi hai.

4.1 Masla kya hota hai

Agar alarm ka system saada bana diya jaye — yaani "jab number limit se upar jaye to alarm baja do" — to aik hi jagah se sainkron alarm aate hain aur operator alarm dekhna hi chhor deta hai. Isay alarm flood kehte hain, aur ye control room ka sabse aam masla hai.

4.2 Is system me kya kya bandobast hai

CheezValueKya karta hai
On-delay6 secondLimit 6 second tak lagatar cross ho, tabhi alarm bajta hai. Aik jhatka alarm nahi de sakta.
Hysteresis4%Alarm khatam karne ke liye value ko limit se 4% neeche aana parta hai. Warna value limit par baithi rahe to alarm baar baar on-off hota rahega.
Off-delay12 second12 second tak theek rahe, tabhi alarm khatam hota hai.
Latching—Alarm khud se gayab nahi hota. Jab tak koi banda "Ack" na kare, screen par rehta hai.
Ringback—Agar kharabi khud theek ho gayi lekin kisi ne dekha nahi, tab bhi alarm list me rehta hai. Warna aane jane wali kharabi kabhi pakri hi na jaye.
Escalation—Agar wahi masla aur bigar jaye to naya alarm nahi banta — wahi purana alarm update ho jata hai.
Re-arm90 secondAik alarm khatam hone ke baad wahi jagah 90 second tak dobara wahi alarm nahi de sakti. Aik kharab sensor se darjnon entries nahi banti.

Aik ahem baat — "Offline" aur "0 kW" alag cheezen hain

Agar meter se rabta toot jaye to dashboard usay "Offline" dikhata hai, "0 kW" nahi. Kyunki inka matlab bilkul alag hai: aik ka matlab hai connection kharab hai, doosre ka matlab hai bijli waqai band hai. Bohot se systems is me ghalti karte hain.

4.4 Har status me rang ke saath icon aur lafz bhi hota hai

Sirf rang par bharosa nahi kiya gaya, kyunki (a) kuch logon ko rang ki pehchan me mushkil hoti hai, (b) print black-and-white me nikle to rang khatam ho jata hai, aur (c) jaldi me lafz parhna rang se zyada tez hota hai.

Saaf baat

Is waqt dashboard par koi bhi live number asli meter se nahi aa raha. Ye aik mukammal, sahi kaam karne wala monitoring system hai jo abhi banaye gaye data par chal raha hai. Software taiyar hai — sirf asli meter lagne baqi hain.

5.1 Asli SCADA se kaise joreinge

Asli meters (Modbus) ↓ Gateway / bridge service ← ye banana parega ↓ (JSON data) /api/stream ↓ Dashboard — koi tabdeeli nahi

Poore dashboard me sirf aik file badalni parti hai (src/source.js) jis me batana hota hai ke data kahan se lena hai. Baqi sab screens khud ba khud asli data dikhane lag jayengi.

Do baatein SCADA jorte waqt zaroor yaad rakhein

1. Agar kisi meter se rabta na ho to us ka data bhejein hi nahi — us ki jagah 0 mat bhejein. Warna "connection kharab" aur "bijli band" ka farq khatam ho jayega.

2. Waqt (timestamp) wo bhejein jo meter par reading li gayi thi, na ke jab data pohancha. Warna atka hua meter bhi theek lagta rahega.

Bab 6

Dashboard Kaise Parhein

Rozana ka tareeqa, aur akhir me zaroori baatein.

6.1 Rozana ka 4-step check (10 second)

  1. Overview ke 6 bare numbers dekhein. Agar Power Factor, Frequency aur Voltage teenon par hara tick hai, to bijli ki quality theek hai.
  2. Active Alarms dekhein. Khali hai to koi jagah limit se upar nahi.
  3. Load ka graph dekhein. Solid line dotted line ke qareeb chal rahi hai?
  4. Upar connection pill dekhein. "Live" likha ho. Agar "Paused" ya "Feed lost" hai to upar wale teen check bekaar hain — data purana hai.

Ye chaar theek hain to aur kuch dekhne ki zaroorat nahi.

6.4 Aage kya karna hai

Is dashboard ko asli system banane ke liye teen cheezen chahiye:

  1. Asli meters — shuru me sirf 3 substation par lagayein. Isi se poora Overview, Voltage aur Power Flow asli ho jayega.
  2. Asli kW ratings — taake limits qabil-e-bharosa hon.
  3. Database (historian) — taake record mehfooz rahe aur proper forecasting ho sake.
Aakhri baat

Software ke lehaz se ye system mukammal aur professional hai. Kami sirf data ki hai — aur wo software ka masla nahi, meter lagane ka kaam hai. Achi baat ye hai ke dashboard khud saaf saaf batata hai ke kaun sa number asli hai aur kaun sa nahi.
